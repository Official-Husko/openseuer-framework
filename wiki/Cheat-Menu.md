Press **Insert** to open the cheats, or open MODS and go to the **Cheats** page. Insert or Esc closes the window. Most cheats need a loaded game.

To use another key, set `Key` in `Mods/CheatMenu/config.json` to a key name such as `Home`, `F2` or `Delete`.

## What's in it

- **Player:** god mode, refill all needs, full heal, noclip, move speed (0.5 to 5 times, remembered between sessions) and three teleport spots per level.
- **Money:** add coins or Real Money, or set both to zero.
- **Items:** give yourself lock picks, bandages, food, tools and some crafting materials.
- **Time and weather:** jump to a time of day, run the clock slower or faster (up to 30 times), and switch the weather where the level has some.
- **Crime:** clear your crime record and wanted level.

## Developer menu

A second window, mostly for testing mods and for quests that got stuck. It's off by default: switch on "Developer menu" under Cheat Menu on the MODS **Mods** page. Then **F8** opens it (or type `devmenu` in the F1 console).

- **Quests:** set a task or one of its objectives to active, finished, failed or abandoned, or move or type an objective's counter. With mods that add quests, show one mod's tasks at a time.
- **Global flags:** look up the game's story flags and switch them.
- **Watches:** follow any value in the game live, also in a small box over the game while you play (**F9** shows or hides it).
- **Player and world:** set any of your stats or skills, go to any entrance of the level or to coordinates, find any item by name, id or mod and give as many as you like, and clear away items of mods you removed.
- **NPCs:** the people around you: go to one, bring one over, make one follow you, look at you or stop what it's doing. Spawn a copy of any character, remove ones a mod added, and set how much people like you.
- **Log:** the game's and the mods' log as it happens, with filters, to see what went wrong without leaving the game.

Anything you change in the developer menu marks the save as changed by it, and the next save carries a small note about that. Mention it if you report a bug from that save.

The full list is in [cheat-menu.md](https://github.com/Official-Husko/openseuer-framework/blob/main/docs/modding/cheat-menu.md).
