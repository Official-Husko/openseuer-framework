Press **Insert** to open the cheats, or open MODS and go to the **Cheats** page. Insert or Esc closes the window. Most cheats need a loaded game.

To use another key, set `Key` in `Mods/CheatMenu/config.json` to a key name such as `Home`, `F2` or `Delete`.

## What's in it

- **Player:** god mode, refill all needs, full heal, noclip, move speed (0.5 to 5 times, remembered between sessions) and three teleport spots per level.
- **Money:** add coins or Real Money, or set both to zero.
- **Items:** give yourself lock picks, bandages, food, tools and some crafting materials.
- **Time and weather:** jump to an hour, run the clock slower or faster (up to 30 times), and switch the weather where the level has some.
- **Crime:** clear your crime record and wanted level.

## Developer menu

A second window, mostly for testing mods and for quests that got stuck. It's off by default: switch on "Developer menu" under Cheat Menu on the MODS **Mods** page. Then **F8** opens it.

- **Quests:** set a task or one of its objectives to active, finished, failed or abandoned, or move an objective's counter.
- **Global flags:** look up the game's story flags and switch them.
- **Watches:** follow any value in the game live.
- **Player and world:** set any of your stats, go to any entrance of the level, find any item by name.

Anything you change in the developer menu marks the save as changed by it, and the next save carries a small note about that. Mention it if you report a bug from that save.

The full list is in [cheat-menu.md](https://github.com/Official-Husko/openseuer-framework/blob/main/docs/modding/cheat-menu.md).
