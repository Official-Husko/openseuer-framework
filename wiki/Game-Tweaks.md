Optional changes to how the game plays. Every tweak is **off until you switch it on** on the **Mods** page of the MODS menu, and its numbers are on the **Settings** page. A tweak that's off doesn't change or cost anything, and nothing a tweak does is written into your save, so you can switch any of them off again later.

| Tweak | What it does | When switched on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Needs | Hunger, thirst, hygiene and each craving grow slower or faster. 0 stops one. | Half as fast |
| Stack size | Bigger (or smaller) stacks, up to 20 times. | Twice as big |
| Spoilage | Food spoils slower or faster. 0 stops it. | Half as fast |
| Crafting speed | Faster or slower crafting, also for furniture at the workbench. | Twice as fast |
| No minimum skill | Recipes can be made whatever your skill, without the low-skill penalty. Skill still helps quality. | |
| Dishwasher speed | Faster or slower dishwashers, on top of the crafting speed. | Twice as fast |
| Renovation cost | Cheaper or dearer renovations. | Half the price |
| Renovations for money only | Renovations don't need materials. | |
| Renovation time | Faster or slower renovations, or instant ones. | Half the time |
| Day length | Longer or shorter days. | Days twice as long |
| Third-person camera | A third-person camera on F6 (change the key on the Hotkeys page), with optional scroll zoom. | |

## Notes

- **Needs** combine with other mods that change them.
- **Stack size:** lowering it again leaves stacks that are already bigger as they are.
- **Spoilage:** freshness already lost stays lost.
- **Crafting speed:** a craft already running keeps the time it started with.
- **Renovations:** an order already under way keeps its time.
- **Third person:** the game itself switches to first person in tight spots, cutscenes and building mode. The camera goes back to third person on its own afterwards, and a small notice says why it switched.

The details are in [game-tweaks.md](https://github.com/Official-Husko/openseuer-framework/blob/main/docs/modding/game-tweaks.md).
