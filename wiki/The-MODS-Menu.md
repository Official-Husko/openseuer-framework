**MODS** is a button in the main menu and in the pause menu. Everything the mods offer is in there, spread over pages. Turn the pages with the `<` and `>` at the top, Page Up and Page Down, the game's tab keys (Q), or the bumpers on a controller.

The menu works with the mouse, the keyboard and a controller. The footer lists the keys.

## Pages

**Mods.** Every installed mod with its version, what it does and whether it loaded. Most mods are made of parts you can switch on and off one by one, like each fix in Unofficial ObenPatches or each tweak in Game Tweaks. Switching takes effect at once, no restart needed.

**Settings.** The settings of every mod on one page, grouped by mod. The search box at the top finds a setting in any of them. A setting you changed has a **Reset** next to its name (Backspace, Delete or a controller's X on the highlighted row does the same), and every mod has a "Reset to defaults" at the bottom of its part.

**Hotkeys.** Every key a mod uses, which you can change to another key or a controller button. If two mods want the same key, the page says so.

**What's New.** What changed in each version, a card per mod, every line marked as new, changed, fixed or removed. It opens by itself once after an update; the UI kit's settings can turn that off.

**Errors.** What went wrong in this session: which mod, what, how often and where. Red means a crash nobody caught, amber a problem a mod noticed and handled. **Make a bug report** takes you to the Report page.

**Report.** Makes a bug report with everything we need. See [Reporting bugs](Reporting-Bugs).

Other mods can add their own pages. The [Cheat Menu](Cheat-Menu), for example, adds a Cheats page.

## Co-op

The main menu and the pause menu also have a **Co-op** button: it opens the co-op window to host, join and find games. Co-op isn't playable yet; see [ObenCoop](ObenCoop).
